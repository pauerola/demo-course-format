HPC for AI - Part 1

Training a computer vision model on Isambard-AI

Author

Jean Golding Institute

Can we teach a computer to spot a fracture?

In short
  • Use Isambard-AI to train an X-ray fracture classifier on a GPU.
  • See what changes when you use four GPUs instead of one.
  • Learn to judge a classifier on imbalanced medical data and to work well on a shared supercomputer.

About this course

Use the menu on the left to move between sections, or the Present switch in the top bar to show a section as slides. The slides used in the session, the notebook and the setup notes are linked at the top of the menu.

Learning outcomes

By the end of this course, you will:

  • Understand when and why AI projects need high-performance computing (HPC).
  • Be able to start a JupyterHub session with GPUs on Isambard-AI.
  • Be able to prepare an image dataset and train an image classification model on a GPU.
  • Know how to judge a classifier on imbalanced medical data.
  • Understand how training a model on multiple GPUs works.
  • Know how to work considerately on a shared supercomputer.
